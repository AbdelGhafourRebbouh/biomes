Make room for your routine.

  1. Open your apps.

    Have the apps you want in your biome open and ready.

  2. Draw their spaces.

    Choose Create biome, draw zones on your screen, then press Enter.

  3. Place your apps.

    Drag each app into its zone, then press Enter again.

  4. Make it yours.

    Give your biome a name, optionally add a shortcut and cover, then save.

Next time, launch your biome from its card or shortcut.
